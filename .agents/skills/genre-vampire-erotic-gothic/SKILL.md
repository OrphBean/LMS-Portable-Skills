# genre-vampire-erotic-gothic

Use when rendering a late-1960s/1970s erotic vampire film pastiche: photochemical 35mm, glamour-grade dark gothic, theatrical chiaroscuro. Pick exactly ONE dominant style family from the Trait schema, at most one secondary, and never blend many. Default family is Hammer Technicolor Gothic (the prose block below).

---

## Style reference block

British Hammer-style Technicolor Gothic, lush Eastmancolor imitating the prestige of Technicolor, late-1960s/1970s 35mm. Saturated arterial scarlet, oxblood, burgundy, plum, violet, dark forest green, candle amber, ivory skin, black velvet, aged gold and cold blue moonlight; reds are narratively privileged as punctuation. Skin stays warm and luminous against cooler, darker rooms. Contrast is medium-high but not crushed; blacks are theatrical while faces and costumes retain readable detail; strong colour separation. Density feels warm and declarative, not faded vintage. Classical 35mm composition organised by arches, columns, bed curtains, stair rails, fireplaces and candelabra; strong foreground/middle/background layering. Slow measured dollies, controlled reframing, push-ins marking recognition or supernatural control; camera at conventional chest/eye height, low angles giving the vampire aristocratic authority. Hard-edged studio moonlight through windows in blue or steel, warm tungsten key on skin often diffused for women, strong backlight separating hair and capes, candlelit rooms brighter than physically plausible. Clear continuity: establishing, blocking, shot/reverse, reaction, then an emphatic violent detail; dissolves for travel/dream. Costumes exaggerate silhouette - high collars, capes, fitted bodices, translucent nightgowns - with colour encoding innocence (white, pale blue) versus corruption (crimson, black, violet). Dense photochemical blacks, period optical softness, mild gate weave. No modern teal-orange grade, no HDR, no extreme shallow depth of field, no contemporary fantasy armour.

## Trait schema

| Attribute | This genre |
| --- | --- |
| Capture | Late-60s/70s 35mm color negative; visible grain, period optical softness, mild halation |
| Grade | Saturated Eastmancolor; warm, declarative, expensive-looking; restrained cyan in shadows |
| Camera | Normal to moderate-wide for rooms; moderate telephoto for glamour close-ups; low angles empower the vampire |
| Movement | Slow dollies, measured pans, controlled reframing; push-ins mark control; handheld rare and meaningful |
| Framing | Classical balanced, layered; women framed as sculptural shapes; architectural enclosure |
| Lighting | Hard blue window moonlight + warm tungsten glamour key + strong backlight; chiaroscuro |
| Production | Artificial ruin-collage sets: castles, manors, crypts; velvet, brocade, candles, ironwork |
| Staging | Theatrical blocking, diagonals, dominant figure standing; two-shots during seduction |
| Editing | Clear continuity; slow seduction, accelerated attack; dissolves for travel/dream |
| Audio | Period score-led; organ/strings; diegetic gate and room sound |

Family (pick exactly ONE dominant, max ONE secondary): Hammer-Technicolor-Gothic, Karnstein-Erotic, Rollin-Poetic-Surrealist, Franco-Psychedelic-Zoom, Belgian-ArtDeco-Decadence, Iberian-Rural-Gothic, Giallo-Inflected, Grindhouse-Sexploitation, Psychedelic-Occult-Mod. Each swaps the grade/color, movement, framing, lighting and editing columns; the full per-family detail and comparison matrix are in `references/<slug>-source.md`. If a second family is used, mark it secondary and keep it confined to one axis (e.g. Rollin's faded coastal grade under a Franco blue structure).

## Exclusions

- No modern dark-fantasy medieval spectacle, no video-game gothic environment.
- No contemporary teal-orange grading, no HDR, no razor-sharp pores, no LED neon.
- No gimbal/drone; no constant handheld; no contemporary-floating smoothness.
- No modern lingerie, cosmetics, or body styling; no high-fashion editorial polish in place of studio-horror theatricality.
- No uniform 'vintage beige' nostalgia grade - period colour was declarative.
- No rainbow palettes (except the one family that calls for it, used as that family).
- No random Dutch angles or frenetic modern montage as the default grammar.

## Example

Hammer Karnstein-cycle erotic Gothic, early-1970s British 35mm Eastmancolor, a candlelit aristocratic bedchamber with crimson velvet canopy and dark carved wood, medium-wide bedside composition framed through lace curtains, elevated three-quarter angle over a reclining woman as a female vampire approaches in profile, restrained slow-dolly feeling, warm tungsten glamour key on luminous ivory skin with hard blue moonlight through the window and a thin rim on hair, palette of cream, blush, burgundy, lilac and arterial scarlet, theatrical chiaroscuro, soft diffusion on candles and white nightwear, moderate film grain, slight halation, period optical softness.
